Survey

Know what you're buying before you commit

A property survey gives you a clear picture of a building's condition before you commit to buying it, helping you avoid costly surprises after you've exchanged contracts. We talk you through the different levels of survey available, from a basic valuation to a full building survey.

Choosing the right level of survey depends on the property's age, condition and construction, and on how much detail you want before proceeding. We help you weigh up the options so you're neither under-surveying a period property nor paying for more than you need on a newer home.

Once a survey is complete, we help you understand the findings in plain terms — what's worth negotiating on, what needs monitoring, and what simply needs noting for the future — so you can move forward with confidence.

  • Help choosing the right survey level for the property
  • Clear explanation of survey findings
  • Guidance on what to raise before exchange
  • Peace of mind before committing to a purchase
